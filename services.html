Drain Specialists

Blocked Drains

Slow draining sinks, gurgling toilets, overflowing stormwater pits — blocked drains are one of the most common plumbing problems. AquaStar uses professional-grade equipment to clear them fast and keep them clear.

We use high-pressure water jetting to blast away blockages caused by grease, debris, tree roots and buildup. Our CCTV camera inspection confirms the drain is fully clear before we leave.

  • Kitchen & bathroom sink blockages
  • Blocked shower & floor drains
  • Toilet blockages
  • Stormwater & sewer drain clearing
  • High-pressure water jetting
  • Tree root removal from drains
  • Drain relining (no-dig repair)

Leak Detection & Repair

Leaking Taps & Pipes

A dripping tap wastes up to 9,000 litres of water per year — and that's just one tap. Hidden pipe leaks can cause structural damage, mould growth and skyrocketing water bills. Don't ignore them.

We use non-invasive leak detection technology to find hidden leaks before they become expensive problems.

  • Dripping tap repair & washer replacement
  • Mixer tap & cartridge repair
  • Leaking pipes under floors & walls
  • Shower & bath leaks
  • Leaking garden taps & irrigation
  • Leaking water meters
  • Pipe repair & pipe relining
